This website may use **Google Analytics cookies**, a web analytics service provided by Google. They are only installed if you click "Accept" in the cookie notice.

## What they are for

They help us understand, in aggregate, how many people visit the website, from which devices and which pages they read most. We use this to decide which content to improve. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## Which cookies are set

- `_ga` and `_ga_*`: tell visitors apart anonymously (they last up to 2 years).

Google may process this data on its servers. More information in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## How to change your choice

You can change your mind at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer, or by deleting cookies in your browser settings.
