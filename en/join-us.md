## Member benefits

- Access to extracurricular activities organised by the AMPA.
- Discounts on selected activities and events.
- Voice and vote at the general assembly.
- Direct updates on everything related to the AMPA and the school.

## How to join

1. Fill in the [membership form](https://forms.gle/mgE2XJYUpSsqGWcLA).
2. Pay the annual fee following the instructions in the form.

## Leaving the AMPA

To cancel your membership, fill in the [same form](https://forms.gle/mgE2XJYUpSsqGWcLA) and say that it is
a cancellation.
