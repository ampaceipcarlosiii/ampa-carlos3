The AMPA (Parents' Association) of CEIP Carlos III is a non-profit
organisation formed by more than 200 of the school's families. Our goal is to represent
families' interests, work together with the school and organise
activities that enrich school life.

## What we do

- We represent families on the school's Governing Council.
- We organise and manage extracurricular activities.
- We help the school with parties and events (Halloween, Christmas,
  Carnival, end-of-year celebration...).
- We manage grants and financial support for member families.
- We keep families informed about school life.
