<h2>What are the benefits of becoming a member?</h2>
<p>Access to the AMPA's extracurricular activities, occasional discounts,
and voice and vote at the general assembly.</p>

<h2>What does the AMPA spend its money on?</h2>
<p>Organising extracurricular activities and school parties, occasional
support for families, and joint projects with the school.</p>

<h2>How can I join the Board?</h2>
<p>Any member can stand. New members are voted in at the general
assembly, which is held at least once a year.</p>

<h2>How do I cancel my membership?</h2>
<p>Fill in the <a href="https://forms.gle/mgE2XJYUpSsqGWcLA">membership sign-up and cancellation form</a>.</p>
