The **AMPA Halloween Party** is coming! Join us on **Friday 30 October, from 5:30 pm to 8:30 pm**, at the school.

There will be plenty of areas for the whole family:

- The Castle Gate (photocall)
- The Vampires' Nest (dining hall)
- The Haunted Passage
- The Ghost Fair (games)
- The Spooky Graveyard (workshops and crafts)
- The Enchanted Café
- Contests and much more…

## Advance tickets

- **AMPA members and collaborators:** €2
- **Non-members:** €3.50

Registration and payment for advance tickets are done by scanning the QR codes on the poster.
